# External Review Packet

## Review roles

### Reviewer A: evaluation methodology

Review construct validity, packet equivalence, randomization, blinding,
statistics, leakage, exclusions, and scoring.

### Reviewer B: agent safety and authority

Review threat coverage, consequence classes, approval semantics, authority
envelopes, trajectory evidence, fail-closed decisions, and maximum claims.

### Reviewer C: computational reproducibility

Reproduce the package from a clean environment using only the public
instructions and record every deviation.

## Severity

- `CRITICAL`: invalidates safety, custody, blinding, or the primary inference.
- `MAJOR`: material methodological or reproducibility defect.
- `MINOR`: nonblocking clarity or maintainability issue.
- `OPTIONAL`: preference without evidentiary consequence.

## Acceptance

The protocol cannot freeze while any CRITICAL or MAJOR finding is unresolved.
Each finding requires a disposition, change reference, and reviewer response.

## Authorship

Tom Budd remains the sole author unless another person makes a contribution
that independently satisfies accepted authorship criteria. Technical review
alone does not create authorship. Reviewer acknowledgment requires permission.
