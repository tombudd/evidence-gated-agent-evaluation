## LinkedIn

I have released a public research candidate: “Evidence-Gated Evaluation of
Agentic AI Systems.”

The central distinction is simple: evidence that an agent can perform a task is
not authority for it to act. The paper turns that distinction into a bounded
evaluation method with frozen acceptance criteria, negative and adversarial
tests, provenance controls, human approval gates, fail-closed behavior, and
explicit capability-claim boundaries.

The current package includes a 12-page manuscript and deterministic synthetic
test materials. It is a release candidate, not a safety certification.
Independent technical review and DOI archival are pending.

Paper:
https://tombudd.com/evidence-gated-evaluation-agentic-ai-systems

Repository:
https://github.com/tombudd/evidence-gated-agent-evaluation

Critical review and clean-room replication are welcome.
