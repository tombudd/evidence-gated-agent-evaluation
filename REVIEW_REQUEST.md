# Independent Technical Review Request

Tom Budd is seeking critical review of this public release candidate. The goal
is to find defects and overclaims before DOI archival, not to solicit an
endorsement.

## Review lanes

Choose one or more:

1. Method and claim-boundary review.
2. Adversarial and negative-test review.
3. Reproducibility and provenance review.
4. Human-approval and authority-boundary review.
5. Statistical design review of the A+ protocol candidate.

## Requested output

For each finding, provide:

- location or artifact;
- severity: blocking, major, minor, or editorial;
- claim at risk;
- evidence or reproduction steps;
- recommended disposition;
- relevant conflict disclosure.

Use `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_FORM.md` or the technical-review issue form. A
clean-room replication should also include the environment, commands, hashes,
and observed results.

## Public disposition

Valid findings will be logged with their disposition. Critical and major
findings must be resolved or explicitly accepted as limitations before the
reviewed DOI release. Reviewer names will be published only with permission.
