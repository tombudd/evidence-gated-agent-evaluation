# Target Reviewer Criteria

Select reviewers for fit and independence, not audience size.

## Minimum portfolio

Aim for at least three invitations across distinct lanes:

1. Agent evaluation, red teaming, or benchmark design.
2. Safety engineering, formal methods, or assurance cases.
3. Human factors, governance, or authorization controls.

## Independence screen

Prefer reviewers who:

- did not draft or implement the artifact;
- can make a public conflict disclosure;
- can evaluate one bounded lane;
- are willing to report negative findings;
- have no expectation of reciprocal endorsement.

Do not describe an invitation, informal comment, or name check as an
independent review. Record a review only after receiving a substantive,
version-linked finding set or signed review form.
