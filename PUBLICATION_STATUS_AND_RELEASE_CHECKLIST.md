## Required before public claims of publication

1. Obtain at least one independent technical review.
2. Obtain an ethics or institutional-review determination before collecting
   human reviewer performance data.
3. Freeze the empirical protocol after independent design review.
4. Repair every valid blocking or major finding.
5. Execute the blinded pilot and simulation-based power analysis.
6. Execute the confirmatory study and clean-room reproduction.
7. Re-run the synthetic suite and regenerate the manifest.
8. Create the dedicated GitHub repository and verified release.
9. Choose a repository license and confirm the current MIT and CC BY 4.0 split.
10. Publish the archival release through Zenodo or OSF.
11. Record the assigned DOI in the manuscript, CITATION file, repository, and
   résumé.
12. Update the manuscript status from release candidate to preprint.
